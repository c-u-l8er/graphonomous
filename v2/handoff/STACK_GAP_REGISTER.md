Classes (from the brief): `SPEC_GAP` (specified, not built) · `IMPLEMENTATION_BUG` · `MISSING_PRIMITIVE` (not even
specified, or refused by ruling) · `INTEROP_GAP` · `PERFORMANCE_GAP` · `NOT_A_STACK_PROBLEM`. **No speculative fix is
presented as completed.** Each row names the owning layer, the evidence (file:line at the pinned revision), the
