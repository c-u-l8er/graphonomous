## 5 · Fit against frozen v0 (`graphonomous.semantic.v0.json` endpoint pairs)

**Fits v0 (no new role/kind/pair)** — REGISTRY node + `CLAIM MEMBER_OF REGISTRY` (208) · `WITNESS WITNESSES CLAIM` (269 assertions on ≤ 269 relations; 87 WITNESS nodes; `WITNESS LOCATED_IN SOURCE_LOCATION`) · `CLAIM ASSUMES ASSUMPTION` (46 typed ASM + 110 free-text; `SCOPED_BY [CLAIM, ASSUMPTION]` is the alternative for the CONDITIONAL antecedent, 3) · `CLAIM BINDS CELL` (54) · `CLAIM SUPERSEDES CLAIM` (14, from 9 `supersedes` + 13 `superseded_by` ids) · `CLAIM CITES *` for `prior_art`/`evidence_qualifiers.citation_ref`/`cites_bound` (SRC-* as **ARTIFACT** — the contract's `cite:` prefix does not exist in `KIND_PREFIX`; `SOURCE_LOCATION` would need a pinned identity a citation lacks) · `status` as `evidence_state {token, vocabulary: "factory-ledger"}` and `obligation`/`evidence_kind`/dates as attributes (contract) · receipts: `RECEIPT PRODUCED_BY ROUND` (20), `ROUND SUPERSEDES ROUND` only if the parent chain is read as replacement (source-stated in `parent.revision`; 9 v3/v4 receipts), `CLAIM PRODUCED_BY ROUND` for `invariants.established` (106/106), `EVIDENCE_STATE_TRANSITION STATE_TRANSITION_OF CLAIM` + `PRODUCED_BY ROUND` for `retyped` (26, prose to parse — defer) · incidents as **FINDING** with `RECEIPT/ROUND OPENS|CLOSES FINDING` (`revision_found`/`fixed_by`, 46) and `FINDING FALSIFIES CLAIM` for the 1 resolving claim subject.

**Does NOT fit v0** (would force a v1 proposal, D-050): `argument → SUPPORTS → claim` — `SUPPORTS` allows only `WITNESS|RECEIPT|FINDING → CLAIM|LAW`, **no `[CLAIM, CLAIM]`**; modelling ARG-* as a CLAIM sub-kind (contract) is refused by the pairs. Defeaters: `ATTACKS` allows only `FALSIFIER → CLAIM|MECHANISM`, so 6 of 68 fit (target_type claim 3 + claim_evidence 3); **62** target an argument/assumption/instrument/receipt/consumption-rule and have no pair. **Recommended smallest v0-safe scope**: (1) REGISTRY + 208 CLAIM (full attrs, `evidence_state`) + MEMBER_OF; (2) WITNESS nodes/edges with `outcome` from status (contract) + LOCATED_IN; (3) ASSUMES (ASM ids; free text under the crosswalk's existing **`text` namespace**, not `assumption:factory:` — the contract's namespace would forbid the cross-registry co-reference D-030 wants); (4) BINDS → `cell:cells:NN`; (5) SUPERSEDES from both fields (one relation, two assertions); (6) CITES → `artifact:factory:SRC-*` and `cites_bound`; (7) ROUND/RECEIPT from the 20 receipts with PRODUCED_BY and `established` → CLAIM PRODUCED_BY ROUND. **Defer**: arguments, defeaters (except the 6 claim-targeted ATTACKS, which can go in as FALSIFIER), incidents-as-FINDING (fits but is a second FINDING semantics — measure first), instruments, objectives, occupancy, operations, embodiment, `retyped` transitions, `derived/`. Verdict: **the core ledger seals under v0; the argument/defeater layer does not** — that is the v1 obligation to propose (`SUPPORTS [CLAIM, CLAIM]` or an ARGUMENT role, `ATTACKS` to ASSUMPTION/WITNESS/RECEIPT).
